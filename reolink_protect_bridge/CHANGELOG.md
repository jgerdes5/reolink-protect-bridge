# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Adoption as a native G4 Doorbell in UniFi Protect
- Live view and continuous recording in high and low quality, with AAC and
  Opus audio (Protect's web player uses WebRTC)
- Ring with push notification and call screen
- Talkback from the Protect app, the web interface and HomeKit
- Motion events and smart detections (person, vehicle, animal, and package on
  doorbells that have it) with bounding boxes
- Detection zones, exclusion zones, line crossing, loitering and privacy masks
- Protect's camera settings applied to the doorbell
- Web interface through Home Assistant Ingress, with discovery of the doorbell
  and the console
